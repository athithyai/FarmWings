## Alignment

The two rasters **share CRS and upper-left origin but are not on the same grid**. The pixel
size ratio is 3.933 and the extents differ by < 3 mm. Identical georeferencing does not prove
identical content, so this was tested:

* Phase correlation of NDVI against RGB darkness on 114 tiles (1024 px) shows a spatially
  varying residual offset: median 4.8 cm, max 3.0 NDVI px (7 cm).
* `processing/align_rasters.py` therefore creates derivatives (originals untouched):
  * `rgb_aligned.tif`: RGB block-averaged onto the NDVI 2.36 cm grid.
  * `ndvi_aligned.tif`: NDVI on the same grid with explicit no-data.
  * `ndvi_coreg.tif`: NDVI warped by a smooth inverse-distance-weighted (IDW) shift field.
    Correlation with RGB darkness rises from 0.565 to 0.623.
* All per-plant NDVI statistics use `ndvi_coreg.tif`. Identification crops are read from the
  original 6 mm RGB.

## Scene content (visual inspection)

A drip-irrigated planting block: 112 thin black drip lines at a **2.0 m** median spacing,
running at 25° to grid north, with planting pits along them. Some stretches of line are
buried or missing. Pits hold young seedlings, often with dry or brown material, and the
planted species is *Rhanterium epapposum* according to the project installation record. Between the lines grows
spontaneous annual vegetation, dense in patches. White plastic debris is visible in places.
